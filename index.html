Got Doubts?

Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers to real queries about HCA, accreditation, LMS access, and time commitments.

Is Humanitarian Code Academy completely free or is there a catch?
It is 100% free with zero hidden fees, subscriptions, or paywalls. HCA was built as an open humanitarian initiative to empower self-taught developers worldwide.
Will I receive an accredited degree or university certificate?
No. HCA is an independent self-learning framework and tracking platform—not an accredited academic institution. We do not issue recognized college degrees. Instead, you graduate with 8 production-grade applications on GitHub to prove your technical abilities to hiring managers.
How does the LMS work if there are no live teachers?
The LMS acts as your digital flight simulator. It guides you week-by-week through quest checklists, curated documentation, project briefs, and accountability logs. You submit live links to verify your completion.
How are humanitarian projects integrated into the coding tasks?
Instead of standard placeholder projects like "Todo App", HCA briefs challenge you to build tools like a Disaster Relief Request Portal, Refugee Supply Inventory Tracker, or Accessibility Audit Suite.
How much time do I need to commit every week?
We recommend setting aside 15 to 18 hours per week to complete reading materials, practice exercises, and project submissions comfortably.
What if I take longer than 12 weeks to complete a semester?
The LMS is self-paced! While the curriculum is structured into 12-week semesters for optimal momentum, your progress is saved locally and in your account book, letting you learn without fear of losing access.
